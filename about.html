Our Story

A house built on Fashion.

Founded in 2019, on a coast that smelled of Samuel.

SOL began as a single linen dress, sewn for a friend's wedding in Nigeria. Today, we are three men working from two small ateliers — one in Osun State, one in Lagos — making heirloom clothing in pieces, not factories.

Every cloth passes through six hands and carries a stylish fit, stitched by the maker.
